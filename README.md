# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple script to calculate simple interest given principal, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Formula
$$\text{Simple Interest} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

Where:
- **P** = Principal amount
- **R** = Annual interest rate (%)
- **T** = Time period (in years)

## Input
- **Principal:** Amount of money invested or borrowed
- **Rate:** Annual interest rate
- **Time:** Time duration in years

## Output
- **Simple Interest:** Calculated interest amount
